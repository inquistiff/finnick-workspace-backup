## Kanban Workflow

### Lane Definitions

| Lane | What It Means |
|---|---|
| `backlog` | Ungroomed. Not yet a commitment. Normal holding state for future ideas. |
| `to do` | Groomed — has acceptance criteria, prioritized, ready to execute. Sean + Tiffany have aligned on it. |
| `in progress` | Actively being worked. |
| `project build` | In active build/development phase (often dev-heavy). |
| `waiting for sean` | Tiffany needs input, decision, or approval from Sean before moving forward. |
| `done` | Shipped. Complete. No further action needed. |

### Backlog Grooming Process

Tiffany and **Sean groom together**. This applies to three backlogs: **RAINMAKER**, **web dev**, and **programs**.

What grooming means:
- Review backlog items together
- Add acceptance criteria (what "done" looks like)
- Prioritize and align on sequence
- Move agreed items from `backlog` → `to do`

**Alexandre's role in grooming:** Do not flag ungroomed backlog items — that's expected. Only flag if items have been moved to `to do` but have been sitting stagnant or are missing acceptance criteria before Tiffany's daily review.

### What Alexandre Should Flag

- Items `in progress` that have shown no movement in 5+ days
- Items `waiting for sean` that have been waiting 7+ days with no update
- `to do` items missing acceptance criteria
- Anything that creates risk to the ≥80% roadmap delivery KPI
- Upcoming release dates with nothing in `project build` or `in progress` stage
- Blockers that Tiffany may not have seen yet (dependency on Ramin, Brennan, Lisa, Paul, Lucia)

### What Alexandre Should NOT Flag

- Backlog items sitting in `backlog` — normal state, groomed on schedule
- `waiting for sean` items that just moved there today
- On-hold items explicitly noted as on hold (Sales Call 2/3 spec, Sprint Workshop Calendar)
