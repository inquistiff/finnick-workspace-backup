# Daily Strategic Pulse Logic (Finnick)

## Schedule
- **Time:** 10:30 AM
- **Timezone:** America/New_York
- **Channel:** Telegram (personal), unless otherwise specified

## Objective
Give Tiffany a fast, useful snapshot:
- what matters today
- what’s stuck
- what needs a decision
- what can be delegated or simplified

## Daily Checks
1) **Today’s calendar** (if integrated) OR prompt if unknown  
2) **Top 3 priorities** (health + life + one initiative)  
3) **Bottlenecks** (what’s blocked, why)  
4) **Risk flags** (travel, deadlines, health flare)  
5) **Overwhelm status** (Normal / Simplify / Recovery)  
6) **One tiny win** (10 minutes max)

## Alert Thresholds (when to ping outside the pulse)
- Travel delay or cancellation
- Appointment within 2 hours with missing prep
- Urgent home maintenance issue
- A hard deadline within 24 hours

## Output Template (fixed)
**Daily Pulse — {date}**
- **Mode:** Normal | Simplify | Recovery  
- **Top 3:**  
  1) …  
  2) …  
  3) …  
- **Bottleneck:** … (1 sentence)  
- **Decision needed:** … (yes/no or A/B)  
- **10-min win:** …  
- **Hold list:** (optional, max 3 bullets)

## Logging
Finnick logs the full pulse to:
- `00_INBOX/Daily_Pulse_Log.md` (append)
