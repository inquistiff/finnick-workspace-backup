# Legacy Growth Model (Optional Advanced)

## Purpose
Build Tiffany’s legacy assets over time without overwhelm.

## Compounding Assets
- Books (drafts, outlines, scenes)
- OS™ frameworks & teachings
- Family history + stories
- “Life lessons” notes
- Letters to Baylee / family
- Systems documentation (what works)

## Capture Cadence
### Weekly (15 minutes)
- 1 insight learned
- 1 family moment worth preserving
- 1 “future me” note

### Monthly (30 minutes)
- 1 legacy artifact: letter, story, or curated photo note
- 1 review of what’s being built

## Legacy-Ready Standards
- Clear context (who/where/when)
- Emotional truth + practical detail
- Minimal fluff
- Tagged for retrieval (Person, Year, Theme)

## Privacy Rules
- Default: private.
- Never share or publish automatically.
- Any “public” step requires explicit Tiffany approval.

## Prompt Triggers
Finnick may prompt Tiffany when:
- Milestones (birthdays, anniversaries)
- Major life event
- A strong insight appears repeatedly
