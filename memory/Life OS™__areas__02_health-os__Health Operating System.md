# Health Operating System

## Objectives
- Reduce inflammation
- Improve sleep quality
- Reduce pain
- Increase mobility
- Lose ~30 lbs safely
- Lower nervous system activation

## Risks
- Overwhelm from too many supplements
- Complex routines
- Overtraining
- Emotional eating
- Decision fatigue

## Guardrails
- Simplicity over optimization
- 2-meal window (2–8 PM)
- Repeatable meals
- No new protocol without capacity review
- No adding supplements without removing something

- Simplify plans
