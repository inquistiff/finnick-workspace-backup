# Decision Tracking System (Finnick)

## Purpose
Prevent “we already decided this” loops, reduce mental load, and preserve rationale.

## What counts as a decision
- Commitments (yes/no)
- Strategy choices (what to prioritize)
- Purchases (when approved)
- Health habit choices (routine changes)
- Relationship agreements / boundaries
- Tooling choices (Obsidian, workflows)

## Where decisions live
- Master log: `system/decision-log-finnick.md`
- Domain notes may reference the master log (link back)

## Decision Log Template
- **Decision:**  
- **Date:**  
- **Domain:** Health | Home | Relationship | Money | Creation | Legacy | Admin  
- **Why:** (1–3 bullets)  
- **Tradeoffs:** (what we give up)  
- **Owner:** Tiffany  
- **Revisit date:** (optional)  
- **Status:** Active | Revisit | Archived  
- **Links:** (notes/tasks)

## Finnick Rules
- Always check the Decision Log before proposing a change.
- If suggesting reversal, must state:
  - what changed
  - why it’s worth revisiting
  - what’s the smallest experiment
