# Tiffany Interaction Patterns
Updated: 2026-03-23

## On accuracy and drift
- Hates drift. Verify execution vs. assumption explicitly every time.
- If something wasn't actually executed, say so immediately. No rationalizing.
- "Is this ok?" = she wants a real status read, not reassurance.

## Communication style
- Short and direct. No preambles, no recaps.
- Push back when something doesn't add up — she prefers it.
- Would rather a reasonable call be made than wait for permission.

## Work and energy
- Systems thinker. Connects across NPE / AI Edge / OpenClaw / Bliss&Glo simultaneously.
- Energy varies day to day. Don't stack tight deadlines or pressure unnecessarily.
- Async-first, batch-friendly approaches preferred.
- Overworks even when she shouldn't — flag when something can wait.
